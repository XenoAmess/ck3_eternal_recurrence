## Existing exact-build source capability

The 1.19.0.6 trace ABI records the selector at RVA `0x33E8D40`; `XarCombatPhaseKnightSelectHookV1` invokes the original exactly once and captures candidate count, returned index, RNG counter/salt before and after, and the selected candidate's two words. The wire deliberately serializes those words as process-local opaque tokens. It does not expose a raw draw, a complete candidate list, a token-to-CharacterID mapping, or a per-call ID for multiple same-side/event selector invocations. Thus a future nonempty row can support only its own bounded count/index and RNG state, subject to the full same-run scope and failure checks. It cannot by itself identify CharacterID 34120 as the selected candidate.

The trace ABI also documents `CCharacter+0x18` full identity and `CCharacter+0x1C8` death marker; `ReadCharacterCoreState` computes `alive = native_valid && !death_marker_present`. The intra-tick ring copies the marker for its prearmed character map. It is not a targeted paused-day CharacterID query. In a02 the final ring read failed after the combat-side removal, leaving that map empty. A distinct read-only targeted query is absent from the current public bridge/driver surface. A new query would need exact-build storage resolution, full-ID equality, a paused same-revision frame, guarded reads, before/after frame identity, and fail-closed missing/stale/fault behavior. Those source and integration tests, an exact Release DLL/injector pair, and independent ABI review do not yet exist as one admitted bundle.
