
opening | 开场与前情：接近四比一，足够判断吗？

[o001] 一千二百八十八对三百三十，接近四比一。只看这一刻的人数，能判断最后谁赢吗？
1,288 against 330: almost four to one. Can the headcount at contact tell us who wins?

[o002] 玩家罗贝尔向阿里开战，争夺阿里治下的塞尔古塞领地；两军却在墨西拿相遇，战斗和战争的账，从这里开始分开。
Robert wages war on Ali for his lands at Siracusa, but they meet at Messina.
The battle and the war have separate ledgers.

[o003] 接战人数只包括已经入场的人。新军加入、骑士受伤或阵亡、败后的追击，会分别改变四本账。
Contact counts those already present. Arrivals, knight injuries or deaths, and pursuit after defeat change four separate ledgers.

[o004] 先看追击：胜负已经分出，损失还在结算。罗贝尔这一侧的面板人数从八百二十四降到七百六十一，哪一笔账在变？
First, pursuit: defeat is decided, but losses are still being settled.
Robert's displayed count falls from 824 to 761. Which ledger is changing?

[o005] 再看骑士：受伤可能改变勇武，阵亡会改变名单。我们把人物状态和普通兵员损失分别核对。
For knights, injury can change prowess and death can change the roster. We check character states separately from ordinary troop losses.

[o006] 接着看新军入场。海上能看见两千五百七十人，但他们加入以后，人数与战宽怎样重算，不能只拿画面整数直接相加。
Next, reinforcements. We see 2,570 troops at sea. Once they join, troop
counts and width are recalculated; simply adding displayed integers will not do.

[o007] 最后看战争分数：这场战斗让玩家记下负五十，战争还在继续。罗贝尔在战斗里是防御方，在战争里却是进攻方。
Finally, warscore: this battle gives the player minus fifty, but the war
continues. Robert defends in the battle while attacking in the war.

[o008] 各章使用独立回放，来源会标在画面上。每一步的前后变化，都只在同一条回放内比较。
The chapters use independent replays, labelled on screen. Each before-and-after comparison stays within its own replay.

[o009] 先看真实界面，再看旁边的原生记录。片中软伤指溃散兵员，硬伤指永久损失；骑士伤势另记。
We start with real UI, then the labelled native records. Soft casualties are routed men; hard casualties are permanent losses. Knight injuries are separate.

[o010] 现在来到追击回放的第二十八日。罗贝尔已是败方，我们从这张暂停画面出发，连续核对接下来的三次结算。
Now enter pursuit on battle day 28. Robert has lost.
Starting from this paused frame, we check the next three settlement ticks.


pursuit | 追击三日

[p001] 追击章使用独立回放甲零五，从第27日检查点重新载入。旧回放提供起点，后续状态以甲零五自己的记录为准。
Pursuit uses the independent A05 replay from a day-27 checkpoint. The older run supplies the checkpoint; subsequent states come from A05 itself.

[p002] 第28日的墨西拿之战已经显示战败，左侧是罗贝尔的撤退中，右侧是敌军的追击中；胜负揭晓后，这笔损失账还没有结完。
On day 28, Messina already shows defeat. Robert is retreating on the left; the enemy is pursuing on the right. Losses are still being settled.

[p003] 追击要分清三列：当前作战人数、溃散兵员，以及永久损失。接下来三次结算，是溃散账减少，永久损失账等量增加。
Keep three columns separate: fighting men, routed men, and permanent losses. These three pursuit ticks move equal amounts from routed to permanent-loss ledgers.

[p004] 软伤这个名称只表示溃散账。现有证据没有查明这些兵员何时治疗、是否归队；真实画面显示的是撤退中。
Soft casualties label the routed ledger. These sources do not establish treatment or return to service; the real screen says retreating.

[p005] 把时间停在第28日，原生记录里败方有二十四个兵团；征召兵和兵士的软伤合起来，是八百二十四点三二二二七人当量。
Freeze day 28. The native record contains 24 retreating regiments, whose levy and men-at-arms soft losses total 824.32227 people-equivalents.

[p006] 人数类原始值按十万倍保存，所以去掉缩放，才得到刚才的人当量；同样带着缩放的追击属性和追击量，却不能直接当成人数。
Count values use a scale of 100,000, which yields people-equivalents after division. Scaled pursuit stats and damage are not themselves headcounts.

[p007] 检验从第28日的完整输入开始：双侧有效属性、两组初始软伤池和修正。算出下一日，再沿模型状态连算三次。
Start from complete day-28 inputs: both sides’ effective stats, two initial routed pools and modifiers. Compute the next day, then continue from the model state.

[p008] 胜方各团的有效追击乘当前参战量，逐项截断后相加，再乘零点五。本案复算的追击量是七千五百二十万三千原始单位。
Multiply each winner’s effective pursuit by fighting men, truncate and sum, then multiply by 0.5. This case gives 75,203,000 raw pursuit units.

[p009] 败方的掩护聚合，是各团有效掩护乘各自溃散兵员后相加。本案结果为零，所以额外分量等于追击量。
Losing-side screening sums each regiment’s effective screen times its routed men. Here that sum is zero, so the extra component equals pursuit.

[p010] 各团的有效坚韧乘各自软伤，逐项截断后相加，得到当天的坚韧加权量。软伤构成因此会影响当天预算。
Effective toughness times routed men, truncated per entry and summed, gives the daily toughness-weighted total. Routed composition therefore affects the budget.

[p011] 模型在这里另算一份保底分量，本案取当天坚韧加权量的百分之五；因此后面有额外和保底两份预算，要分别保留整数截断。
The model also computes a floor component, here five percent of that day's weighted toughness. Extra and floor budgets retain their separate fixed-point truncations.

[p012] 本次合并修正系数是零点七五；每份分量先除当天坚韧加权量，再乘第28日冻结的对应软伤池和修正，最后按三日分摊。
The combined modifier is 0.75. Each component is divided by today's weighted toughness, multiplied by its frozen day-28 soft pool and the modifier, then divided by three.

[p013] 征召兵与兵士各有一组冻结的初始软伤池，各算额外和保底预算。先求这组当天的转入量，再分配到兵团。
Levies and men-at-arms have separate frozen initial pools and extra/floor budgets. Calculate each domain’s daily conversion before allocating it to regiments.

[p014] 分摊时，用各团当天软伤在同组软伤里的比例，算出它承担的部分；这让兵团大小进入结果，而不是二十四团各领一笔相同损失。
Allocation uses each regiment's share of its group's current soft losses. Regiment size affects its loss, rather than assigning all 24 regiments the same amount.

[p015] 比例运算逐步截断后，原版第二遍按存储顺序处理规定的分配余数；所以对拍必须保留顺序和整数步骤，不能只用浮点算一个总数。
After the proportional operations truncate, the native second pass handles the specified allocation remainder in stored order. Exact comparison preserves these integer steps.

[p016] 比如五十号团，首日有一点零七七二人当量转入硬伤；它的软伤相应减少，次日可读的硬伤栏也增加同量，两列分别核对。
For example, regiment 50 converts 1.07720 people-equivalents on the first tick. Its soft field falls and its readable hard field rises by the same amount.

[p017] 现在揭开第29日实际画面，左侧面板人数变成八百零三；首日逐团记录汇总，软伤转成硬伤二十点七零六七七人当量。
Reveal the actual day-29 frame: the left panel now shows 803. The first tick's per-regiment records sum to 20.70677 people-equivalents converted from soft to hard losses.

[p018] 首日二十四项软伤变化、二十三项可读硬伤变化，都与下一帧原版逐项相同。零差覆盖的是这些字段。
All 24 soft changes and 23 readable hard changes match the next native frame on tick one. Zero difference refers to those fields.

[p019] 还有一个兵团的硬伤字段在回读中是空值，不能把空值当作零；因此每一天都只对二十三项可读硬伤报零差，保留这项缺口。
One regiment's hard-loss field is unavailable, which is not zero. Each day's hard-loss parity covers only the 23 readable fields and retains this gap.

[p020] 第二次结算接着模型算出的败方状态走。第29日的原版读数只用于核对，不替换模型的答案。
Tick two continues from the computed losing-side state. Day-29 native readings are comparisons, not replacements for the model’s state.

[p021] 第30日的真实面板是七百八十二，这次新增硬伤二十点九七四七三人当量；二十四项软伤和二十三项可读硬伤，再次逐项零差。
The day-30 panel shows 782. This tick adds 20.97473 people-equivalents of hard losses, with all 24 soft and 23 readable hard changes again matching exactly.

[p022] 第三次同样接着模型状态走，第31日面板来到七百六十一；新增硬伤二十一点二六一一九人当量，可比较的二十四项和二十三项仍然全对。
The third tick carries the model state onward. Day 31 shows 761; hard losses increase by 21.26119 people-equivalents, and the 24 soft and 23 readable hard changes all match.

[p023] 第28到31日是四个暂停状态、三次跨日结算。画面依日期排列，损失按三次结算相加。
Days 28–31 provide four paused states and three daily ticks. The frames follow dates; losses sum the three ticks.

[p024] 三次精确新增量合计六十二点九四二六九人当量；累计七十二项软伤变化、六十九项可读硬伤变化，都与这条原版回放逐项相同。
The three exact increases total 62.94269 people-equivalents. All 72 soft-loss comparisons and 69 readable hard-loss comparisons match this native replay.

[p025] 三天的追击量相同，败方软伤构成和坚韧加权量却在变化，所以每日新增损失略有不同，不能把首日结果直接乘三。
Pursuit stays constant, but routed composition and toughness-weighted totals change. Daily losses therefore differ; multiplying day one by three would miss this.

[p026] 初始软伤池保持第28日的冻结值，当天分摊则使用已经减少的软伤；把这两种口径分开，才能让后两天的预算和逐团分配继续对上。
The initial pools stay frozen at day 28, while allocation uses the reduced current soft pools. Keeping these distinct lets later budgets and regiment allocations continue matching.

[p027] 这三次结算，败方二十四个兵团的当前作战人数都没有改变。面板少了六十三，变化落在溃散与永久损失账。
Across these ticks, fighting men in all 24 losing regiments stay unchanged. The 63-person UI decrease accompanies changes in routed and permanent-loss ledgers.

[p028] 四个面板整数，分别对应本案软伤总量去掉小数后的数值；但界面的内部取数公式还没有证实，这种对应关系只按本案观察保留。
Each UI integer matches the truncated soft-loss total in this case. The UI's internal formula remains unverified; we retain this as an observed correspondence.

[p029] 给定甲零五第28日的状态，三次追击的软伤和可读硬伤可以连续算准。结论只覆盖这组输入。
Given A05’s day-28 state, three ticks of soft and readable hard losses match in sequence. This conclusion covers those inputs.

[p030] 非零败方掩护仍缺实机验证。公式已知，但还没有掩护实际生效的同帧输入与次日逐团账，零掩护样本不能覆盖这一分支。
Nonzero losing-side screening still lacks live parity. The formula is known, but active same-frame inputs and next-day regiment ledgers are missing.

[p030a] 掩护小于追击时，额外分量取两者差；掩护达到或超过追击时，额外分量归零，保底分量还要比较最低门槛。
When screen is below pursuit, extra is their difference. At or above pursuit, extra is zero; the floor component still checks a minimum.

[p030b] 要补验，需要败方确有非零有效掩护与软伤，采齐同帧属性、修正和冻结池，再对次日逐团软硬伤。两种分支都要各有原版样本。
Live validation needs losing entries with positive effective screen and routed men, same-frame stats, modifiers and frozen pools, plus next-day ledgers for both branches.


knights | 骑士事件：名单、属性与死亡写回

[k001] 第二本账看骑士。要分别看人物是否留在名单，以及他此刻的勇武；人数和属性会有不同的变化。
The second ledger concerns knights. Check whether a character remains on the roster and his current prowess; headcount and attributes can change differently.

[k002] 这份独立画面有十一名防御方骑士。第五行若弗鲁瓦勇武七，上面一名同名人物勇武十二；同名仍需分辨身份。

[k003] 右侧的七是勇武，名单标题的十一才是骑士人数。这张画面只有当前状态，没有展示这名骑士此前的属性。
The seven on the right is prowess; eleven in the heading is the knight count. This image shows his present state without showing his earlier attributes.

[k004] 骑士战斗力作为倍率参与计算：伤害等于有效勇武乘五十再乘倍率，坚韧等于有效勇武乘十再乘倍率。百分之一百是一倍。
Knight effectiveness is a multiplier: damage = effective prowess × 50 × E; toughness = effective prowess × 10 × E. At 100%, E = 1.

[k004a] 例如百分之一百七十五就是一点七五倍；每一点有效勇武，对应八十七点五伤害和十七点五坚韧。后面用实际骑士核算。
At 175%, E = 1.75. Each effective prowess point gives 87.5 damage and 17.5 toughness. We next check these against an actual knight.

[k005] 接下来用历史回放零三九与零四零说明致残。它们保留了原生记录与同源存档，画面旁的解释卡据此制作。
Historical replays 039 and 040 explain maiming through retained native records and linked saves. The adjacent explanatory cards are based on those sources.

[k006] 零三九的新增战报中，若弗鲁瓦，编号三四三三三，被穆罕默德，编号四七零三二，致残；若弗鲁瓦仍然存活。
Replay 039 adds a maiming report: Geoffroy (34333), maimed by Muhammad (47032). Geoffroy survives.

[k007] 源档已有一条受伤战报，本日又新增一条致残。累计两条，今天新增一条，要用前后差集区分。
The source save already contains an injury report; this day adds one maiming report. The cumulative list has two, but the daily increment is one.

[k008] 若弗鲁瓦新增独腿与受伤特质，保存中的基础勇武仍是三。下面另看原版重新求出的有效勇武。
Geoffroy gains One-Legged and Wounded; saved base prowess remains three. Next we examine the effective prowess evaluated by the game.

[k009] 他的对手穆罕默德，编号四七零三二，获得一百五十威望；成长抽签选中空条目，基础勇武仍是十。
His opponent Muhammad (47032) gains 150 prestige. The growth draw selects the empty entry; base prowess stays at ten.

[k010] 零四零重新载入零三九的第六日存档，字节完全一致，也没有推进日期。它提供伤后状态的独立回读。
Replay 040 reloads 039’s exact day-six save without advancing the date. It independently reads back the post-injury state.

[k011] 两侧合计仍有二十四名骑士、五十一条兵团记录。若弗鲁瓦的六十一号兵团仍记一人，改变的是属性。

[k012] 若弗鲁瓦的有效勇武从十一降到七，骑士战斗力保持百分之一百七十五，即一点七五倍。存档基础勇武仍为三。

Toughness also uses E: 11 × 10 × 1.75 = 192.5, then 7 × 10 × 1.75 = 122.5. Headcount stays constant while stats fall.

[k016] 伤害是战斗属性，不能念成每天杀死同样人数。其他兵团也有正常伤亡，所有人数差不能都归给这次致残。
Damage is a combat stat, not an equal number of daily kills. Other regiments also suffer ordinary casualties; this maiming does not explain every headcount difference.

[k017] 再看本轮独立击杀回放：图尔吉塞，编号三三四三七，死于阿姆鲁，编号三四一二零。起点是十二月二十九日检查点。

[k019] 原版删掉不合格项时，用尾项填进空位，再检查当前位置。我们保留了每次交换后的顺序，不能把它改成稳定删除。
Stock filtering fills a failed item with the tail and checks that position again. Every resulting order is retained; stable deletion changes that order.

[k020] 索引从零开始，八对应第九项。最终数组的第九项是阿姆鲁，编号三四一二零，与战报击杀者一致。

Native counters, salt and returned index are recorded. The draw reconstructed with this build’s algorithm has remainder eight modulo fourteen; the draw value is derived.

[k025] 阿姆鲁仍存活，面板勇武仍为七，威望三百零一变四百五十一。写入函数和存档确认，威望货币与累计威望都增加一百五十。

[k026] 成长发生在选定击杀者之后、死亡提交之前。原版给阿姆鲁威望，再执行独立的成长抽签，成长与击杀各有自己的记录。

[k026b] 六十这一项，先减击杀者学识的两倍；若王朝有对应军事传承，再乘零点五。勇武加一这一项，脚本权重保持三十。
The no-growth weight starts at 60 minus twice the killer’s Learning, then halves with the specified warfare dynasty legacy. The +1 prowess entry remains at 30.
