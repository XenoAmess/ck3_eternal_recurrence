A genuine head/digest and persisted-reference change disabled reuse for the remaining calls. Retired key/point/quota/result slots, including slot15, now stay zero; slot0 still describes the original scoped Driver decoder.

No private Root or physical qualification is represented. Necessary Loader-only and changed-Test typing passed without rebuilding other production or support code.
