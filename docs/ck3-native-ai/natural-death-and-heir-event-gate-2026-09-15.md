## Distinct natural scenes

| Gate | Natural scene | What can be concluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G2-M2 death_management.1007 | The living played ruler's current heir dies, the ruler is close or extended family of that heir, and another player heir exists when the notification opens. | A source-reviewed single event choice, typed selection, event-instance advance, independently read player-stress change, and next formal turn consuming the closed event. |
| G2-M3 | The played ruler dies naturally and CK3 changes the played character to the actual heir in the same campaign. | Frozen per-title first-heir expectation versus inherited titles, matched predecessor-estate reconciliation, real-successor continuation and gameplay/checkpoint after the transition. |

The two gates cannot be proved by the same death. Vanilla on_death queues hidden death_management.0001. Its title-holder collection at death_management_events.txt:340-363 includes a holder only if holder.player_heir equals the dying character. Hidden death_management.0002 at lines 653-695 chooses prisoner, house-arrest and battle variants first; its ordinary heir branch sends .1007 only if the holder is close or extended family. The .1007 event at lines 2004-2006 requires a replacement player_heir, and its only option at lines 2038-2044 applies minor_stress_impact_gain. There is no government trigger on this chain, so an ordinary standard-feudal campaign may encounter it naturally, but an arbitrary heir death does not guarantee this exact event.

By contrast, tgp_travel_events.0030 at tgp_travel_events.txt:368-377 explicitly requires both the TGP DLC trigger and celestial_government. It cannot naturally occur while the played ruler remains standard feudal. Keep that G2-M2 requirement open for a separate naturally encountered celestial bounded scene; forced triggering in the feudal campaign would be only local development evidence.
