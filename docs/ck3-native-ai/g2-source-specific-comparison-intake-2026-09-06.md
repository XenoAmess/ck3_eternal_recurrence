# G2 source-specific comparison intake

Status: **static-ready / no-launch postprocessor; live input pending**.

## Delivered seam

The concrete source-loss lifecycle already produces one action-bound result
containing the six `bookmark.1071.a` source executions, same-process current
regiments, one surrender, destroyed cleanup and two equal persisted-expiry
reads. Its report previously stopped at `source_specific_loss_ready=true` and
had no adapter into `raiktor-three-way-exit-policy-v1`.

`prepare_g2_source_specific_comparison_intake.py` now validates that report as
one indivisible chain:

- all observer, bridge and lifecycle PIDs are identical;
- the outer owner performed exactly one final cleanup and released its launch
  slot after the lifecycle;
- the source set, retention ticket and postwar receipt share the same WarID,
  connection generation, episode and generation hash;
- the source-specific join has every check GREEN and exactly the three known
  remaining providers;
- the only mutation is `surrender-war-<full-generation WarID>`;
- cleanup is `destroyed`, source-bound pre-termination soldiers become the
  exact surrender-boundary loss, and the persisted truce comes from the native
  row rather than a formula.

Only after those checks does the adapter project
`raiktor-observed-surrender-outcome-v1` with
`source_specific_attribution_ready=true`. The generic postwar receipt itself
continues to carry `source_specific_attribution_ready=false`; source identity
belongs to the separate six-execution join and cannot be inferred from generic
cleanup.

The postprocessor now sends that validated projection through the unified
`raiktor-three-way-exit-intake-provider-v1`. Its output retains both the full
`three_way_intake_result` and the existing `three_way_policy_result`
compatibility field. This removes manual provider composition while keeping
campaign, owner-budget and white-peace inputs explicitly unavailable and all
production/action gates false.

## No-launch boundary

The adapter reads a caller-supplied report whose SHA-256 must be provided
explicitly and writes a new deterministic JSON intake. It does not enumerate,
start, attach to, focus, mutate or terminate CK3. The already frozen live
adapter and its manifest are unchanged, so this addition does not invalidate
the pending exclusive live command.

Focused normal and optimized tests cover the qualified projection, the
generic-receipt/source-join distinction, PID and readiness drift, overclaims,
and exact report-hash binding.

## Remaining decision boundary

A qualifying future report will make the historical source-specific surrender
outcome policy-consumable. It still cannot select or submit an exit because the
pre-action comparison continues to require:

1. `campaign-dominance-certificate`;
2. `owner-authored-budget-profile`;
3. `same-frame-white-peace-comparison-certificate`.

Therefore `three_way_comparison_ready`, `decision_ready`,
`automatic_surrender_ready` and `gen034_closed` remain false. No CK3 action or
readiness promotion is delivered by this static package.
