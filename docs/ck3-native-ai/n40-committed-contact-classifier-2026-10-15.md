The previously installed classifier expected exactly one enemy and required battle at the final target. It rejected this valid two-enemy, first-hop observation after the already executed action. The [scoped FINAL03→FINAL04 patch](../../tools/g2_saved_campaign/patches/n40-classifier-final03-to-final04.patch) compares the complete hostile roster and both war summaries with the selected war, Native H1 first hop, conflicts, stationary enemy routes, physical combat state and original sentinel actions. Applying to a different classifier base is unsupported.
