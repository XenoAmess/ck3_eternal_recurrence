The queue installed classifier FINAL02 at sequence 12 and reconsumed the original AUTO11 result at sequence 13. That reconsumption recognized the readback with zero new construction start, material, or completion credit and did not submit a game action. The active queue must not be proactively replaced merely for this follow-up. The next planned warm queue uses immutable FINAL03, SHA-256 `d7d9c55d55ebe9aefc9bafef0650e479461626cb836e8a6fa979bdbb6d938d8d`; its only added restriction lets an untracked, same-process construction monthly watch fall through to the existing strict classifier.

The permanent pure rule is [`construction_cold_readback_classifier_v1.py`](../../tools/g2_saved_campaign/construction_cold_readback_classifier_v1.py). It accepts only a previously tracked, applied construction with exact old action identity, a fresh actor/episode-bound current Game frame, and a read-only receipt whose progress, province income and intent all agree with that frame. It records the current generation separately without rewriting the old action binding. A monthly watch requires its due date and increasing proof epoch; native completion can earn completion credit once, while a repeat income read cannot. Rejected or unknown evidence remains a failure, not business credit or permission to replay. A new action with no cold-generation tracker stays on the original same-process rule.

The [reduced AUTO11 fixture](../../tools/g2_saved_campaign/fixtures/construction_cold_readback_p02_0011_minimal.json) records the immutable response SHA-256 `89f06870d3fd57f8098a8bb11e4d92f619e89f2cdd2e4c4993d6719c3a8a9732`. Its state contains only the original action fields reconstructed from the receipt, rather than claiming to be a full governance-state snapshot. [Focused tests](../../tools/g2_saved_campaign/test_construction_cold_readback_classifier_v1.py) cover the actual cold receipt, future due monthly readback, one-time completion, stale or mismatched proof, attempted new submission and the untracked same-process fallback. The future monthly and completion receipts in those tests are synthetic; real N40 completion is not yet claimed.

## P04 same Game readback correction

The completed P04 AUTO13 response (SHA-256 `bf2e6f7cf9a08dd917556c75159176367595645334a21fc0d3c41ec151710fe4`) exposed two representation changes in a later monthly read. The same Game process birth appeared as `20261015043736.520398+000` in the retained tracker and `20261015123736.520398+480` in the fresh receipt; both denote the same UTC microsecond. A same Game SDK rotation also restarted the SDK-local public revision counter at 12 after the prior readback's 46, while native revision advanced from 47 to 114 and the proof epoch from 232479 to 596597. Literal birth-string equality and cross-SDK public-revision monotonicity incorrectly rejected that read.

The pure classifier now parses complete DMTF CreationDate values to exact UTC instants and records the current connection generation. A public revision decrease is accepted only with a newer connection generation and strictly newer native revision, date, and proof epoch; an older tracker without a connection field uses the same Game birth and those strict freshness checks once, then records the observed generation. The fresh receipt's public revision must still equal its current planning source. PID, actor, episode, original construction identity, native observations, and no-new-submission checks remain required. This is a readback; it grants no new construction start or completion credit. The [focused portable tests](../../tools/g2_saved_campaign/test_construction_creation_offset_v1.py) cover equivalent offsets, SDK counter reset, legacy trackers, and wrong PID, birth, frame or proof. The external actual-response replay passed; this paragraph does not claim that the pure classifier had already been installed when written.
