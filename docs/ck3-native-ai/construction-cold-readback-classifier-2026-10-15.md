That reconsumption recognized the readback with zero new construction start, material, or completion credit and did not submit a game action. The active queue must not be proactively replaced merely for this follow-up. The next planned warm queue uses immutable FINAL03, SHA-256 `d7d9c55d55ebe9aefc9bafef0650e479461626cb836e8a6fa979bdbb6d938d8d`; its only added restriction lets an untracked, same-process construction monthly watch fall through to the existing strict classifier.

The permanent pure rule is [`construction_cold_readback_classifier_v1.py`](../../tools/g2_saved_campaign/construction_cold_readback_classifier_v1.py). It accepts only a previously tracked, applied construction with exact old action identity, a fresh actor/episode-bound current Game frame, and a read-only receipt whose progress, province income and intent all agree with that frame. It records the current generation separately without rewriting the old action binding. A monthly watch requires its due date and increasing proof epoch; native completion can earn completion credit once, while a repeat income read cannot. Rejected or unknown evidence remains a failure, not business credit or permission to replay. A new action with no cold-generation tracker stays on the original same-process rule.
