## 协调者执行的最小切换

```powershell

  'Z:\ck3_mod_rewrite\artifacts\migrations\2026-09-30\post-update-1.20.0.2\production-source-97883fd\ck3_autonomous_player\mcp_server.py' `
  --driver native-headless `
  --state-dir 'C:\Users\xenoa\AppData\Local\XarAutoplayer-1.20.0.2-migration' `
  --pipe-name '\\.\pipe\xar_ck3_bridge_migration_12002' `
  --transport stdio
```
