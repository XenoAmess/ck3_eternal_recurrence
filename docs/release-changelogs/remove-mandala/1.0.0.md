## Player-visible additions

- Adds one game rule, `肃清曼荼罗伪信`, enabled by default with the requested description.
- Converts every Mandala ruler to Wanua at game start, after one day, and through a persistent monthly event sweep.
- Converts every Temple Citadel to a Castle at game start, after two days, and through the yearly global pulse.
- Intercepts successful AI government changes into Mandala through the shared government-change hook and immediately redirects the AI ruler to Wanua, covering decision, event and scripted-effect conversion paths that reach that hook.
- Ships English, Simplified Chinese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian and Spanish localization.
- Ships a purpose-made 640×640 thumbnail and two real CK3 acceptance screenshots on the public Workshop page.

## Compatibility and save migration

- Built and tested for CK3 `1.19.0.6` and the game content that defines Mandala/Wanua governments and Temple Citadels.
- A new single-player game is recommended. Enabling the rule in an existing save intentionally rewrites matching governments and holdings on its next scheduled sweep.
- Changing a holding type may remove buildings that are incompatible with a Castle; this is expected CK3 behavior.
- Mods that replace the same government, holding, game-rule, event or on-action keys are not guaranteed compatible.

## Known limits

- Live recurring-path acceptance invoked the exact monthly and yearly dispatchers on accelerated fixture dates rather than waiting a wall-clock in-game year.
- The live run proves global-zero postconditions for the loaded 1066 world and one legal successful AI transition, not every possible third-party conversion implementation.
- Seven additional release languages are reviewed model-generated candidates, not native-speaker certification.
