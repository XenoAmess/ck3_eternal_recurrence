## Player-visible additions and changes

- Adds a default-off automatic defensive-call toggle. When the player is declared on, the mod calls every AI helper that the applicable vanilla interaction can bring in for zero gold, prestige and piety: eligible allies, guaranteed suzerain/tributary relationships, eligible regents and free house members. Paid dynasty or tributary calls are skipped, overlapping relationships are deduplicated, and replay is idempotent.
- Adds a bulk conversion decision with a real 0–100 slider. It sends vanilla conversion demands only to eligible AI characters in the player's realm whose acceptance value meets the selected threshold, then reports accepted and rejected counts in a toast.
- Adds two bulk hook-payment decisions: one requires the target to afford the full vanilla obligation; the other accepts targets with more than one gold and collects their current integer gold. Both retain vanilla eligibility and consume the hook.
- Adds two bulk ransom decisions: one requires the applicable full ransom; the other accepts a willing payer with at least one gold and takes the currently available integer gold.
- Adds priority-aware bulk conditional release. For every willing prisoner it maximizes the compatible subset of gain-hook, recruit and conversion terms, breaking equal-size ties in that order.
- Keeps the 1.0.2 automatic-appointment and vassal-transfer toggles unchanged and independently controllable.
- Extends all nine shipped localization structures for phase two. Simplified Chinese and English are the reviewed baselines; the other seven translations passed format and terminology audit but do not claim native-speaker approval.

## Compatibility and save migration

- Built and tested for CK3 `1.19.0.6`; single-player remains recommended.
- No save migration is required. The automatic defensive-call feature is off until explicitly enabled; bulk actions run only when the player chooses their decisions.
- Bulk actions and defensive calls retain their corresponding vanilla interaction eligibility, war, religion, imprisonment, relationship and cooldown restrictions.
- The appointment feature still replaces three vanilla files under `common/succession_appointment/` and remains incompatible with mods that replace the same paths.

## Known limits

- Automatic calls are dispatched one in-game day after the defensive war start so the vanilla war and interaction context is available.
- The two partial-payment modes use integer gold and intentionally exclude the boundary below their documented minimum: hook payment requires more than one gold; ransom requires at least one gold.
- Seven additional release languages are reviewed model-generated candidates, not native-speaker certification.
