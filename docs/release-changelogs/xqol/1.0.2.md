## Player-visible additions

- Adds two independent, default-off decisions for human independent administrative, meritocratic and celestial rulers.
- “自动选择继任” removes the human top liege from subordinate appointment succession without changing the vanilla candidate pool, eligibility rules or relative scores among other candidates.
- “别把封臣给我” prevents subordinate administrative rulers from using the vanilla AI transfer path to push unwanted vassals to the player; player-initiated transfers remain available.
- Both features can be disabled independently, restoring vanilla behavior and removing only flags owned by this mod.
- Ships English, Simplified Chinese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian and Spanish localization.
- Uses the exact Chinese product name `XenoAmess的体验优化` and a purpose-made 640×640 Workshop preview.

## Compatibility and save migration

- Built and tested for CK3 `1.19.0.6`; a new single-player game is recommended.
- The appointment feature replaces three vanilla files under `common/succession_appointment/` and is incompatible with mods that replace the same paths.
- Existing saves do not require a data migration. Both features remain off until the player explicitly enables them.

## Known limits

- Live acceptance covers one real celestial-government death sample, one removal sample and the transfer-guard lifecycle; exact-byte validation covers all five modified appointment types.
- Seven additional release languages are reviewed model-generated candidates, not native-speaker certification.
- The mod targets the three supported government types and does not alter other succession systems or player-initiated vassal transfers.
