## Player-visible additions

- Adds the default game rule “Fate of the Chinese Hegemon / 中华霸权统治者的命运”, with “Reclaim the Motherland / 重整河山” and a vanilla-compatible shattering option.
- During Chaos, the old ruler loses the Chinese Hegemony but keeps every county personally held before shattering and every unrelated personal title.
- Creates an empty-de-jure Later-Dynasty Hegemony such as “Later Song / 后宋”.
- Keeps only direct landed Pro-Hegemon vassals under the old ruler while preserving each loyalist’s existing subordinate realm tree; other direct vassals continue through vanilla Chaos reorganization.
- Prevents a Later-Dynasty holder from using the original “Claim the Mandate / 宣称天命” decision.
- Adds “Proclaim the Restoration / 宣称复辟” at the same live territorial threshold as the original mandate decision: currently 51% of the Chinese Hegemony’s de-jure counties, including qualifying tributary land.
- Restoration invokes the complete vanilla mandate effect and then destroys every Later-Dynasty Hegemony held by the restorer.
- Ships English, Simplified Chinese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian and Spanish localization.
- Ships a purpose-made 640×640 thumbnail and three real CK3 screenshots; the first is centered on the Great Song capital at Kaifeng.

## Compatibility and save migration

- Built and tested for CK3 `1.19.0.6` and requires `Crusader Kings III: All Under Heaven`（《溥天之下》）.
- Start a new single-player game. Existing saves are not a supported migration target for the new game-rule branch.
- The mod overrides exactly `tgp_chaos_shattering_effect` and `situation_dynastic_cycle_claim_mandate_decision`. Mods replacing either key may conflict by load order.
- The alternative game-rule setting preserves the original Chaos behavior for comparison and compatibility.

## Known limits

- Multiple historic Later-Dynasty titles may coexist; another warlord claiming the Mandate does not automatically destroy an unrelated Later-Dynasty title.
- Custom player-authored dynasty names use the engine’s native moved-title-name and prefix behavior; 89 vanilla dynasty names have explicit deterministic localization combinations.
- Seven additional release languages are reviewed model-generated candidates, not native-speaker certification.
- The Steam page’s three screenshots are cropped/encoded projections of one GREEN acceptance run. The AI-generated key art is only the thumbnail and is not presented as gameplay.
