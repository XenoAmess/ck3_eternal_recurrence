## Player-visible additions and changes

- Adds the game rule “The Choice of the Loyalists / 尊王诸侯的抉择”. Its default “Divided Hearts / 人心离散” lets direct landed Pro-Hegemon lords reconsider their allegiance at the moment of Chaos; “Unwavering Loyalty / 誓死尊王” retains the 0.1 behavior.
- Adds hard-defect conditions for open war, deep enmity, disloyalty, very low opinion, and independence or claimant faction membership; clear personal and dynastic bonds can hard-lock loyalty when no open break exists.
- Other AI candidates make one frozen 5%–95% loyalty roll based on opinion, relationships, personality, rank, relative military strength, imperial legitimacy and dread.
- Lords who remain keep the same primary title object, displayed or custom realm name, direct liege and complete subordinate realm tree. A loyal “Qingxu Circuit / 青徐路” no longer passes through the vanilla weak-title pruning or rename path.
- Defectors and all other direct vassals continue through the vanilla Chaos reorganization.
- Adds the player-facing “Divided Hearts / 人心向背” summary after shattering.
- Keeps the complete 0.1 restoration loop: personally held land and unrelated titles survive, the Later-Dynasty Hegemony is created, Claim the Mandate is blocked for its holder, the original 51% threshold unlocks Proclaim the Restoration, and successful restoration destroys the Later title.
- Updates all nine release languages and adds a fourth real CK3 screenshot to the checked-in Workshop BBCode.

## Compatibility and save migration

- Built and tested for CK3 `1.19.0.6`; requires `Crusader Kings III: All Under Heaven`（《溥天之下》）.
- Start a new single-player game. Existing campaigns cannot retroactively change the game rules frozen at game start.
- The new rule defaults to Divided Hearts. Unwavering Loyalty is the explicit compatibility setting for the 0.1 loyalist behavior.
- The mod still overrides exactly `tgp_chaos_shattering_effect` and `situation_dynastic_cycle_claim_mandate_decision`; mods replacing either key may conflict by load order.

## Known limits

- The synchronous Chaos effect cannot reliably pause and wait for several multiplayer clients one by one. Human Pro-Hegemon candidates therefore use the same frozen factors and a public deterministic 50-point boundary; 0.2.0 has no per-client choice popup.
- This release validates deterministic mechanism cases, not a statistically representative natural-campaign loyalist population. It does not claim a fixed post-shattering land-share average or hidden balance cap.
- Multiple historic Later-Dynasty titles may coexist, and another warlord claiming the Mandate does not automatically destroy an unrelated Later-Dynasty title.
- Seven additional release languages are reviewed model-generated candidates, not native-speaker certification.
