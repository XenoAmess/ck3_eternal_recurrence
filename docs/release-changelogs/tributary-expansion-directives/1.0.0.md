## Player-visible changes

- Adds a player-only Expansion Directive character interaction for a direct AI
  tributary and lets the suzerain choose one eligible neighboring foreign
  county.
- Charges 150 Prestige for every valid order. The suzerain's Prestige level
  also affects the tributary's acceptance score.
- Offers an optional war subsidy equal to 12 months of the tributary's income,
  rounded up to 5 Gold and clamped to 50–500 Gold. Gold moves only after a
  valid acceptance.
- On acceptance, starts one dedicated county conquest with the tributary as
  primary attacker and the selected ruler as defender. Victory grants the
  county to the tributary, not the suzerain.
- A valid refusal still spends 150 Prestige and retains the five-year
  per-tributary cooldown, but transfers no Gold and starts no war. A
  same-frame invalidation refunds Prestige and removes the cooldown.
- Includes complete English, Simplified Chinese, French, German, Japanese,
  Korean, Polish, Russian, and Spanish localization.

## Compatibility and migration

Target game version: CK3 1.19.0.6. No DLC or other mod is required. Existing
saves may enable the mod, but a backup is recommended before changing the mod
set. Mods that replace the same character-interaction or casus-belli behavior
may change compatibility. Normal CK3 truce, alliance, tributary-network,
herder, inheritance, and war restrictions remain authoritative.

## Known limitations

The two formal live cells do not claim production interaction-selector UI
coverage, a same-frame invalidation/cooldown-refund execution, completed
victory/white-peace/defeat outcomes, save/reload, or a measured post-war
tribute delta. Those paths are source-contract checked and exact-parser loaded
where applicable, but remain explicit live-coverage gaps.
