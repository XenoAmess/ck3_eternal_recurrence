# 宗族化密教 / Dynastic Esotericism 0.2.3

适用 CK3 1.21 Florin / 1.21.0.3。

## 变更

- 全世界周期兜底改为每年一次：集中维护、将所有在世支系成员迁回固定宗家及适用的新建宗族家主校正。包含玩家、AI及其他信仰或礼仪成员；不改死者谱系。取消持续月度全扫及逐人物分散全年扫描。
- 出生和人物生成保留知识处理，次日同步；创建、编辑、改礼仪和死亡保留事件处理。实际同礼仪领袖获任头衔只处理家主，不重扫整礼仪。旧月度任务只清旧记录，不再扫描或续排。
- 合并批量关系更新，保留已有背离关系及已赦免、已消耗的罪行理由，不清空后重建。需要完整信徒—背离者关系时，其规模仍存在；不承诺CPU、FPS或墙钟改善倍数。
- 修复未启用本模组时创建的旧档加载后没有自动初始化的问题；无需重开，原手动入口保留。只补缺失选择与合资格人物知识；普通礼仪补“无”，不强制宗族化，已有选择和绑定不重建。新局神职校正不借给旧档。
- 整个有效绑定宗族的AI通过已覆盖原版入口创建支系受限，包括异信仰成员；玩家保留原条件。无需新支系的商人路径、合法家主转交保持。该保护不是第三方或引擎原语的统一拦截。
- 年度归宗使用稳定宗家锚点；旧档来源歧义、失效或绑定冲突时不随意迁人，解除宗族化后停止。宗家不随当前家主或支系变化重选。
- 仅本产品新建宗族的宗家家主，跟随绑定礼仪实际存活、同礼仪与信仰的领袖，在创建、适用领袖变更及年度维护时校正。复用旧宗族、异礼仪共享教宗及原待处理操作不误改。家主和引擎宗族族长分别观察，不把设置家主等同于设置族长。
- 繁育教派的三个信条改为肉欲高扬、祖先崇拜、追逐权力；葬礼改为制为木乃伊。固定繁育礼仪完整旧三信条与天葬组合只迁移一次，自定义与后续编辑保留；白绮不改，原版信条选择资格不放宽。
- 原生创建／首次改革的族长复用原宗族、家族及纹章；其他合格者新建，必要时保留真实成年创始人。背离的固定-100好感、罪行理由、管辖与赦免保护、RRF注册和默认接口、加载顺序均保留。

## 本轮实际范围

在1.21.0.3自然跨年后的保存中，目标宗族56名在世成员均处于固定宗家，包括玩家和跨礼仪AI；宗家家主与实际所属礼仪领袖一致，对照宗族保持。结果在跨年后的自然日保存中确认，不据此填写元旦瞬时执行时间或回调先后次序。另一个31自然日窗口未见月度续排；标记消失本身不证明旧任务执行次数。

独立重新载入后，在不推进日期的情况下再次保存，56名在世成员逐人仍在同一宗家，宗家家主、实际所属礼仪领袖及对照宗族状态保持。

创建界面全部路径、即时领袖继承／头衔转交后的跟随、全产品／多人／长期及实测性能倍数不在本轮新增通过结论中。家主、族长及宗教领袖保持实际身份分列，不以候选人物代替领袖。

## English

Yearly upkeep covers world-wide maintenance, recovery of all living cadet members and applicable new-dynasty house-head correction. Players and members of other faiths or rites are included; dead genealogies are unchanged. Births and new characters retain knowledge handling and next-day synchronization. Creation, edits, rite changes and deaths remain event-driven. Relevant title gains update only the house head; old monthly tasks drain without scanning or renewing.

Bulk work preserves existing apostasy relationships and pardoned/consumed reasons. Pre-mod saves initialize automatically without restarting, retaining the manual fallback, existing choices and bindings. Ordinary missing choices receive None; new-game clergy correction is not reused on old saves.

Covered vanilla paths restrict AI cadet creation throughout a validly bound dynasty, including other faiths; player rules and paths not requiring a new cadet house stay intact. Annual recovery uses the stable main-house anchor and leaves ambiguous or invalid legacy identities untouched. Only mod-created dynasties follow their actual living same-rite leader as main-house head. Reused dynasties, foreign shared Popes and pending operations stay protected; House head and Dynast remain separate observations.

Procreation uses Carnal Exaltation, Ancestor Worship and Pursuit of Power, with Mummification. Only its intact legacy three-tenet/Sky Burial bundle migrates once; custom and later edits, Vivhite and vanilla picker rules are preserved. Existing founding/reuse, retained-adult-founder, -100 apostasy opinion/crime/jurisdiction/pardon rules and RRF interfaces/load order remain.

On 1.21.0.3, a save after naturally crossing the year boundary confirmed all 56 living members of the tested dynasty in its fixed main house, including the player and an AI of another rite. The house head matched its actual own-rite leader, with the control dynasty preserved. This does not establish instant New Year dispatch or immediate head-transfer behavior. A separate 31-day window showed no monthly renewal, without deriving exact old-task counts from marker absence. No CPU/FPS/wall-clock multiplier, all-creation-path, whole-product, multiplayer or long-term pass is claimed.

An independent reload followed by a save without advancing the date preserved the same 56 living members individually in the main house, the actual own-rite leader/main-house-head agreement and the control dynasty.

## 历史边界 / Historical scope

Earlier bounded 0.2.2 first-reform/two-save-day checks and 0.2.1 CK3 1.20 evidence keep their original scopes. Earlier failures and untested post-inheritance editing remain separate.
