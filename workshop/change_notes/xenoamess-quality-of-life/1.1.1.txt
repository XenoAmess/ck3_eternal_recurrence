XenoAmess的体验优化 1.1.1 — CK3 1.20 维护候选（发布前草稿）
相对上一公开版本 1.1.0。

本次源码维护候选为 1.1.1，兼容目标声明为 CK3 1.20.0.3；该版本独立实机验收与正式发布仍待完成。本文件不是已发布或全部功能已通过的收据。

本次变更：
• 从当前已审原版重建行政、贤能与天朝三份任命继承投影，只保留既有玩家候选扣分；保留新版公共计分、候选等级与冷却。
• 禁止向上转封改用新版行政机制判断，仍限定本 mod 的 guard 所有权；关闭后不移除其他来源的保护状态。
• 批量改信与条件释放适配新版接受度、天朝等级及原生礼仪/国教礼仪上下文；显式绑定玩家 puppet_or_actor，保留原版后果与宗教拒绝门禁。
• 修正足额牵制索款的报价上下文：逐目标绑定 recipient，并用未被钱包封顶的原版报价检查能否足额支付；实际交易、强牵制规则、消耗与取整仍由原版互动执行。
• 普通赎金采用新版 normal_ransom_cost_value；劫掠报价、真实付款人重定向、实时钱包检查和释放仍沿原版路径。只处理金钱支付，不自动选择 herd 等其他支付方式。
• 修正朝贡防御召援的合同方向：宗主保证援助朝贡者，不表示朝贡者必须反向参战。向下自动召援现要求朝贡者负有强制参战义务；已由原版自动加入的目标不会重复召集。
• 将手写 effects 按职责拆分，补独立报价值文件，发行树由 24 增为 27 文件；既有业务 ID、默认关闭的三开关、批处理入口及九语文案保持。

兼容与存档：本候选没有新增必须执行的存档迁移步骤，更新前保留备份。自动继任仍覆盖 common/succession_appointment 的三份 governor 文件，与同路径覆盖 mod 不兼容。现有款牵制索款仍要求目标金钱大于 1，现有款赎囚仍要求愿意付款者至少 1 金钱；所有目标仍须满足原版战争、宗教、囚禁、关系与冷却条件。
验收边界：1.20.0.2 的天朝、行政及受影响付款场景已有有界实证，原失败与日志归因保留。本次 1.20.0.3 新输入已准备付款与原防御矩阵，但尚未实机，不能替代五种任命候选、复杂防御、全部滑条边界、宗教负门禁及完整 GUI 矩阵；正式发布、缓存复验与 changelog 入库均 pending。

XenoAmess Quality of Life 1.1.1 — CK3 1.20 maintenance candidate (pre-release draft)
Changes relative to the previous public version, 1.1.0.

The source maintenance candidate is 1.1.1 and declares CK3 1.20.0.3 as its compatibility target. Separate live acceptance on that build and formal publication remain pending. This is not a publication receipt or a claim that every feature has passed.

Changes:
• Rebuilds the Administrative, Meritocratic and Celestial appointment projections from reviewed vanilla definitions, keeping only the existing player-candidate penalty and retaining new shared scoring, candidate tiers and cooldowns.
• Uses the current administrative-mechanic check for the vassal-transfer guard and preserves ownership of this mod's protection state; disabling it does not remove another source's guard.
• Adapts bulk conversion and conditional release to current acceptance modifiers, Celestial tiers and native Rite/state-Rite context. It binds puppet_or_actor to the player and retains vanilla consequences and religious refusal gates.
• Corrects full hook-payment quote context: binds each recipient, then checks affordability against the vanilla quote before the final wallet cap. Vanilla still performs the transaction, strong-hook handling, hook use and rounding.
• Uses normal_ransom_cost_value for ordinary cash ransoms, retaining the raiding quote, real payer redirect, live-wallet checks and vanilla release. Other payment modes such as herd are not selected automatically.
• Corrects the direction of tributary defensive obligations: a suzerain guarantee protects the tributary, rather than obliging it to defend the suzerain. Automatic downward calls now require a forced-participation obligation, and targets already added by vanilla are not called again.
• Splits handwritten effects by purpose and adds a separate quote-value file, taking the release inventory from 24 to 27 files. Existing effect IDs, three default-off toggles, bulk-action entries and all nine localization texts are retained.

Compatibility and saves: this candidate adds no mandatory migration procedure; preserve a backup before updating. Automatic appointments still replace the three governor files under common/succession_appointment and conflict with other replacements at those paths. Partial hook payment still requires more than one gold; partial ransom requires a willing payer with at least one gold. Vanilla war, faith, imprisonment, relationship and cooldown eligibility still applies.
Acceptance limits: bounded Celestial, Administrative and affected-payment evidence exists for 1.20.0.2, with original failures and log attribution preserved. The newly prepared 1.20.0.3 inputs cover payments and the original defense matrix but have not been run; they do not certify all five appointment branches, complex defense, every slider boundary, religious negative gates or the full GUI matrix. Formal publication, fresh-cache verification and permanent changelog delivery remain pending.
