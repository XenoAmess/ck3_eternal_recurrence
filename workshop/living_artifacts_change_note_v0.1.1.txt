0.1.1 — 它是活的 / It is alive

新增
• 活武器的宝物说明首行新增【它是活的】，下方保留原有说明全文。
• 新觉醒的活武器在刷新时获得标记；旧存档中的活武器在下次刷新或月度处理时补上，包括已带★名称和未装备的物品。
• 标记仅追加一次；普通宝物不受影响。
• 新增首行已补齐全部九种语言。

兼容与范围
可继续使用现有存档；暂停状态下尚未经过刷新或月度处理的旧物不会立即更新。成长、经验、异能、诅咒、喂养与金币数值沿用0.1.0。

Changes
• Living artifacts now begin their description with 【It is alive】, followed by the complete original description.
• Newly awakened artifacts receive the marker on refresh. Existing living artifacts receive it on their next refresh or monthly processing, including starred and unequipped items.
• The prefix is applied once and ordinary artifacts are unaffected. The new line is included in all nine localization files.

Compatibility
Existing saves remain supported. Paused older items update when their next refresh or monthly processing occurs. Growth, experience, powers, curses, feeding and gold values remain unchanged from0.1.0.
